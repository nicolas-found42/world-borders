# World Borders

The language of the historical border globe and its evidence.

## Language

**Polity**:
A historically identified political entity whose identity is separate from its display name and territory.

**Boundary state**:
Geometry assigned to a polity, representation, and supported time extent.

**Snapshot**:
A source-supported boundary state for a particular calendar year.

**Validity interval**:
An explicitly evidenced start-inclusive, end-exclusive period for a boundary state. Neighboring snapshots do not establish an interval.

**Boundary event**:
A reviewed change affecting named polities, with supporting evidence and stated date precision.

**Representation**:
The kind of assertion a boundary state expresses, such as legal affiliation, effective control, territorial claim, or dispute.

**Coverage gap**:
The absence of published eligible geometry for a requested region, time, and representation.

**Reference geometry**:
A licensed generalized outline that has not completed independent historical boundary validation.

**Evidence item**:
A preserved source passage or map reference with provenance and the specific assertion it supports.

**Judgment**:
A typed Jev answer with probabilities, versioned inputs, and a recorded disposition.

**Curator disposition**:
A recorded decision on a boundary state’s publication eligibility, including scope, limitations, and unresolved conflicts.
